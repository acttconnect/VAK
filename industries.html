Target Sectors

Who We Design For

From standard ready-mix plants to deep mining operations, our assets perform in tough conditions.

Ready Mix Concrete (RMC)

Supplying heavy 100T-200T cement/flyash silos equipped with high throughput 219mm screw conveyors and safety relief systems.

Road Contractors & Builders

Supplying modular silo systems on mobile steel skid platforms, allowing quick assembly and relocation without foundation costs.

AAC Block Manufacturers

Advanced flyash and lime storage silo solutions configured with fluidizing aerators for continuous block line feeds.

Flyash Brick Manufacturers

Medium capacity silos configured for ash collection and automatic mixing plant integration, optimizing raw flow rates.

Precast Concrete Industry

Integrated silo and scale setups offering high weighing accuracy for prestressed beam and girder manufacturing.

Crusher Mining & Quarries

Providing high-durability Knap-ex woven wire meshes, anti-clog wave grids, magnets, and heavy-duty idler rollers.

Water Treatment Plants

Custom lime and soda ash storage silos with dust filtering and automated discharge feeding setups.
