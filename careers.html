Sales & Business Development

Sales Executive

Bhopal / Indore Office
01 POSITION

Job Profile

We are seeking a proactive B2B Sales Executive. The candidate will coordinate client inquiries for heavy bulk cement silos, flyash silo systems, conveyor screws, and aggregate screen wire meshes, interfacing with RMC plants and builders.

Key Responsibilities

  • Develop relationships with road contractors, RMC plant owners, and quarry operators.
  • Formulate pricing quotes and explain technical specifications such as Twin-Lobe vs Tri-Lobe and screw angles.
  • Conduct client visits and offer post-sales lifecycle coordination.

Requirements

  • Graduate in Business Administration, Engineering, or related field.
  • Strong communication skills in English & Hindi. B2B sales experience preferred.
