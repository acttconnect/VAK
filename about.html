VAK Group Overview

VAK Group stands as a premier manufacturing and industrial engineering force in Central India, specializing in high-durability material handling and advanced bulk storage infrastructures. Established in 2013, we have scaled operations to become a leading manufacturer and supplier of heavy-duty solutions, including Cement and Fly Ash Silos, high-durability woven wire mesh screens, Bulker Feeding Root Blowers, and precision silo accessories.

Driven by our core Tri-Pillar Mindset (V-A-K), we deliver exceptional Value through cost-effective, long-lasting assets; maintain a proactive Attitude to solve complex heavy engineering challenges; and deploy deep technical Knowledge across every deployment. Every product is engineered at our state-of-the-art production facility, utilizing quality-approved materials and undergoing rigorous testing to ensure strict compliance with standard IS quality norms. By combining structural innovation with sustainable engineering, we build foundations that optimize modern industrial operations and cement our clients' construction dreams.

Operations Showcase

Factory & Workshops

Take a look inside our state-of-the-art production environments, workshops, and testing labs.

Mandideep Factory & Bhopal Unit

Our primary manufacturing plants in Mandideep and Bhopal are equipped with automatic welding systems, coil cutters, and automated painting booths to eliminate leakages, paint peeling, and structural rusting.

  • Full proof automated factory-welded joints
  • PU Paint coating application booths
  • Advanced steel cutting & bending machinery

Metal Mesh & Screen Panel Workshop

Dedicated section for designing and manufacturing screen panels, anti-clog wire meshes, Knap-ex screens, and polyurethane screen systems for mineral screening and aggregate crushers.

  • Specialized weaving looms for lock-crimp and flat-top styles
  • High tensile spring steel wire raw materials
  • Custom aperture size tolerances strictly controlled

Woven Mesh Production Lines

Our heavy-duty weaving section handles heavy wire diameters, creating wear-resistant screening products used by major mining groups and quarry operators across Central India.

  • Manganese steel and high carbon wire options
  • Anti-Clog wave configurations (A & B styles)
  • Reinforced hooks for quick screen tensioning

Laboratory & Quality Testing

Strict metallurgical and stress-testing processes. Every batch of steel plates, wire rolls, and accessories undergoes comprehensive inspections to guarantee field durability.

  • Tensile strength testing for spring steel wires
  • Weld penetration ultrasound checking for silo shells
  • PU paint thickness (DFT) checks
